\subsubsection{Bees Knees}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent\textbf{\LARGE Bees Knees\index{Bees Knees}}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.02\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \raggedleft
    \textbf{\large Gin}\\
    \sweettartguage{0.2}\index[drinks]{Tart!20\%!Bees Knees}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Flavor Profile:} Honey\index[flavor-profile]{Honey}, Citrus\index[flavor-profile]{Citrus}, Herbal\index[flavor-profile]{Herbal}, Refreshing\index[flavor-profile]{Refreshing}.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large History:} The Bees Knees is a cocktail that was created during Prohibition with ingredients that would mask poor tasting bathtub gins. The original recipe called for equal parts, but nowadays our gin is delicious, and the recipe is now tweaked to highlight the gin. "Bees Knees" was an expression to highlight how amazing something was, and this cocktail was a favorite during Prohibition.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Ingredients}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Gin 60ml\index[ingredient]{Gin}
        \item Lemon Juice 22,5ml\index[ingredient]{Lemon Juice}
        \item Honey Syrup 22,5ml\footnote{Honey Syrup: 3 parts honey, 1 part hot water. Stir and let come to room temperature. Simple!}\index[ingredient]{Honey Syrup}
    \end{itemize}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Garnish}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Lemon Twist\index[ingredient]{Lemon Twist}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.01\textwidth}\vrule\hspace{.01\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Tools/Glassware}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Shaker
        \item Hawthorne Strainer
        \item Jigger
        \item Y-Peeler
        \item Stemmed Cocktail Glass\index[glass]{Stemmed Cocktail Glass}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Instructions}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Place all ingredients into a shaker.
        \item Add ice to shaker and shake your drink for 8--10 seconds.
        \item Double strain into stemmed glass.
        \item Garnish with lemon twist.
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Fun Tip:} If this drink has too much honey flavor, try changing your honey syrup ratio to 2:1 or even 1:1.
