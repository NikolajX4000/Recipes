\subsubsection{Tiramisu Cocktail}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent\textbf{\LARGE Tiramisu Cocktail\index{Tiramisu Cocktail}}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.02\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \raggedleft
    \textbf{\large Dessert}\\
    \sweettartguage{-0.4}\index[drinks]{Sweet!40\%!Tiramisu Cocktail}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Flavor Profile:} Coffee\index[flavor-profile]{Coffee}, Sweet\index[flavor-profile]{Sweet}, Rich\index[flavor-profile]{Rich}, Chocolate\index[flavor-profile]{Chocolate}, Savory\index[flavor-profile]{Savory}.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large History:} Tiramisu is the most popular dessert in the world, and it was only a matter of time until a cocktail would be created to match the flavor of this delicious coffee cake treat. Adam Ennis is credited for creating this cocktail at the Restaurant Isola in London, England in 2001. This cocktail not only includes an egg yolk, but has the addition of mascarpone cheese and could serve as its own dessert!
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Ingredients}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Cognac or Brandy 30ml\index[ingredient]{Cognac}\index[ingredient]{Brandy}
        \item Kahlua 15ml\index[ingredient]{Kahlua}
        \item Crème de Cacao 15ml\index[ingredient]{Crème de Cacao}
        \item Heavy Cream 30ml\index[ingredient]{Heavy Cream}
        \item Egg Yolk 1\index[ingredient]{Egg Yolk}
        \item Mascarpone Cheese 1 tbsp\index[ingredient]{Mascarpone Cheese}
    \end{itemize}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Garnish}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Chocolate Powder\index[ingredient]{Chocolate Powder}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.01\textwidth}\vrule\hspace{.01\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Tools/Glassware}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Shaker
        \item Strainer
        \item Jigger
        \item Small Coupe\index[glass]{Small Coupe} or Coffee Cup\index[glass]{Coffee Cup}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Instructions}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Place all ingredients into a shaker.
        \item Add ice and shake for 15--20 seconds.
        \item Remove ice and shake again for 30 seconds (reverse dry shake).
        \item Strain into glass.
        \item Garnish with chocolate powder.
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Helpful Tip:} The reverse dry shake allows you to thicken your cocktail with longer shaking but without dilution.
