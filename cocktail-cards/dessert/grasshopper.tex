\subsubsection{Grasshopper}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent\textbf{\LARGE Grasshopper\index{Grasshopper}}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.02\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}{.49\textwidth}
    \raggedleft
    \textbf{\large Dessert}\\
    \sweettartguage{-0.5}\index[drinks]{Sweet!50\%!Grasshopper}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Flavor Profile:} Sweet\index[flavor-profile]{Sweet}, Minty\index[flavor-profile]{Minty}, Chocolate\index[flavor-profile]{Chocolate}, Creamy\index[flavor-profile]{Creamy}.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large History:} The Grasshopper was reportedly created by Philibert Guichet, the owner of New Orleans famous Tujague's restaurant. The cocktail was created for a New York City cocktail competition and placed second in 1919. The following year Prohibition started and as a result many cocktails were not documented. However, a 1919 newspaper had the cocktail listed for its second-place prize. Tujague's is still known for its signature Grasshopper cocktail.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Ingredients}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Crème de Menthe (green) 30ml\index[ingredient]{Crème de Menthe}
        \item Crème de Cacao 30ml\index[ingredient]{Crème de Cacao}
        \item Heavy Cream 30ml\index[ingredient]{Heavy Cream}
    \end{itemize}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Garnish}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Ground Nutmeg\index[ingredient]{Nutmeg} or Chocolate\index[ingredient]{Chocolate}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.01\textwidth}\vrule\hspace{.01\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.49\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Tools/Glassware}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Shaker
        \item Strainer
        \item Jigger
        \item Coupe\index[glass]{Coupe} or Martini Glass\index[glass]{Martini Glass}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Instructions}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Place all ingredients into a shaker.
        \item Add ice and shake for 15--20 seconds.
        \item Double strain into coupe or martini glass.
        \item Garnish with fresh ground nutmeg or chocolate.
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Helpful Tip:} For a boozier cocktail add 30ml of brandy. For a boozy milkshake add a scoop of vanilla or chocolate mint ice cream and serve in a larger glass.
