\noindent
\textbf{\large Flavor Profile:} Wine\index[flavor-profile]{Wine}, Fruity\index[flavor-profile]{Fruity}, Dry\index[flavor-profile]{Dry}, Refreshing\index[flavor-profile]{Refreshing}, Tannin\index[flavor-profile]{Tannin}, Boozy\index[flavor-profile]{Boozy}.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large History:} Sangria was made by early Greeks and Romans who would add spices, sugars, and local fruits and vegetables to their wine. The term "Sangria" can be traced back to 18th century Spain, where it has been a staple of the culture. It was introduced to the U.S. at the 1964 World's fair in New York City, and Americans have been thirsty for Sangria ever since! Made in batches you can customize your Sangria with all your favorite fruits and berries.
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{.48\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Ingredients}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Spanish Red Wine 2 bottles\index[ingredient]{Red Wine}
        \item Brandy 177,5ml\index[ingredient]{Brandy}
        \item Orange 2 (1 diced and 1 juiced)\index[ingredient]{Orange}
        \item Green Apple, diced 1\index[ingredient]{Green Apple}
        \item Lemon, diced 1\index[ingredient]{Lemon}
        \item Cinnamon Stick 1\index[ingredient]{Cinnamon Stick}
    \end{itemize}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Optional,} one of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Simple Syrup\index[ingredient]{Syrup}
        \item Soda water\index[ingredient]{Soda Water}
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\hspace{.01\textwidth}\vrule\hspace{.01\textwidth}
\begin{minipage}[t]{.48\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Tools/Glassware}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Pitcher\index[glass]{Pitcher}
        \item Bar spoon
        \item Measuring cups
        \item Juicer
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\large Instructions}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item In a pitcher add diced fruit.
        \item Add juice from 1 orange and 1 cinnamon stick.
        \item Add brandy and wine.
        \item Stir with bar spoon and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
        \item Pour into glass of your choice and top with soda water (optional).
    \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\vspace{.5em}\hrule\vspace{.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{\large Helpful Tip:} Try white or rose for a lighter sangria. Also feel free to add your favorite fruits and berries to customize your own sangria!
